Privacy Policy for Mull

Last updated: November 2026

What data Mull collects

Nothing. Mull has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no internet connections of any kind. No name, email address, IP address, location, voice data, or any other personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored by the developer.

Microphone access

Mull requests microphone permission solely to record the voice notes you explicitly start. Recording begins only when you start it and stops when you pause or finish. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Speech recognition and AI

Transcription and summaries are generated on your device by Apple's on-device technologies (SpeechTranscriber and Apple Foundation Models). Your audio and transcripts are never sent to the developer, because the developer has no servers. Note that Apple may, as part of its own system services, download speech recognition assets to your device; this is handled entirely by iOS and involves no audio or transcript content leaving your device.

Storage on your device and in iCloud Drive

Recordings, transcripts, and app settings are stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Drive enabled, Mull stores your transcripts in your own private iCloud storage so they sync across your devices — this is your personal iCloud space, governed by Apple's privacy terms. The developer has no access to it.

Sharing and export

When you share or export a transcript, the file is generated on your device and handed to the iOS standard share sheet. You choose the destination. Mull has no knowledge of where it goes.

Children

Mull does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recording in the app removes it completely. Uninstalling the app removes all app data on the device. Anything already in your iCloud Drive is managed by you under your Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

Contact
